Mobile Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Privacy in our games

This policy explains what information is processed when you download and play mobile games published by Hope Technologies (“we”, “us”, “our”) on Google Play, why it is processed, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Our website has a separate privacy policy.

At a glance

  • Each game is different. The Games covered table shows exactly which games have ads, analytics, other Firebase services or in-app purchases.
  • Our games do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Game progress, settings and the items you buy are saved on your device.
  • Games with ads use Google AdMob, and some games use Google Firebase for crash reports, game settings or analytics. These services receive device information and identifiers such as your advertising ID or a Firebase installation ID. Games without them do not.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See Deleting your data.

3. Information we process

In all games:

  • Information stored on your device: game progress, unlocked levels, in-game items such as coins, stars, hints and lives, and settings (such as sound and vibration). We do not receive this information. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Technical information: our games are built with the Unity game engine. The engine and Google Play may process limited technical information, such as device model, Android version, app version and crash or performance data, to run the game and keep it stable.
  • Information you give us: if you email or call us for support, we receive your email address or phone number, your message and anything you attach.

Only in games with ads, Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform also collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, language, app version and network type.
  • Identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Approximate location: your general region, worked out from your IP address. We never collect GPS or precise location.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and rewarded ads completed.
  • Consent choices: in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, the choices you make in Google’s consent form.

Only in games with in-app purchases (Google Play Billing):

  • Purchase confirmation: when you buy something, Google Play confirms the purchase to the game, and the game stores what you own on your device. Google Play processes the payment, and the game never sees your card or payment details. See In-app purchases.

Only in games that use Google Firebase, as shown in the table above:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the game crashes, a report is sent with the crash stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and a Crashlytics installation ID.
  • Game settings (Firebase Remote Config): the game downloads its settings from Firebase using a Firebase installation ID.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), only in games marked “Yes” for analytics: gameplay and usage events such as levels started and completed, session length and taps, linked to an app-instance ID. No current game uses analytics.

4. How we use information

  • To run the game and remember your progress, settings and purchases.
  • In games with ads: to show ads, including rewarded ads that unlock in-game bonuses, measure them, prevent ad fraud, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
  • In games with in-app purchases: to give you what you buy and restore it from Google Play.
  • In games with Crashlytics: to find and fix crashes.
  • In games with Remote Config: to download the latest game settings.
  • In games with analytics: to understand how players use the game so we can improve it.
  • To answer support and privacy requests, and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and consent

This section applies to games with ads. Today that is Parcel Fit: Pack the Box, which shows banner, interstitial, rewarded and app open ads from Google AdMob. Its ads are set up for a general audience and are not child-directed. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID where the law allows it and you have not opted out.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice at any time from “Privacy choices” in the game.
  • All players: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). On older Android versions, you can reset it or opt out of ads personalization there. You will still see ads, but they may be less relevant.
  • United States: where state law gives you the right to opt out of targeted advertising, use the Android setting above or contact us.

7. In-app purchases

This section applies to games with in-app purchases. Today that is Parcel Fit: Pack the Box, which offers Remove Ads, Unlock Everything, a Starter Pack and star packs through Google Play Billing.

  • Payments: Google Play processes every payment. The game never sees your card or payment details.
  • What the game receives: only a confirmation of your purchase from Google Play.
  • What is stored: what you own is stored on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the game can restore your purchases from Google Play.

10. Children

Our games are designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A game’s content rating on Google Play, such as “Rated for 3+”, describes how suitable its content is for different ages. It does not mean the game is designed for children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us at hopesoftworks@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Data retention

  • On your device: game progress, settings and the purchases you own stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Purchases can be restored from Google Play.
  • Analytics (Firebase): user-level event data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase settings, no longer than 14 months, and then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): kept for about 90 days.
  • Game settings (Remote Config): Firebase installation IDs are kept by Google as described on the Firebase privacy page.
  • Advertising (AdMob): kept by Google according to Google’s retention policy.
  • Purchases (Google Play): purchase records are kept by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Support emails: kept for as long as needed to resolve your request, normally no longer than 12 months.

12. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. We extend these choices to all players.

To delete your data:

  1. Uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android settings. This removes progress, settings and the record of your purchases stored on your device. You can restore purchases from Google Play later.
  2. In games with ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings so new activity is not linked to your previous ID. In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, you can also change your ads consent from “Privacy choices” in the game.
  3. To ask us to delete analytics and crash data, support emails or anything else, email hopesoftworks@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”, the name of the game and the approximate date you last played. You do not need an account.

We will reply within 30 days. Because we do not link game data to your name, we may ask for details that help us find it. If you are unhappy with how we handled your request, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Security and international transfers

Data sent by our games to the services above is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We protect our developer and analytics accounts with strong security controls. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information. Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.
