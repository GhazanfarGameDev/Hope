Mobile Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Privacy in our games

This policy explains what information is processed when you download and play mobile games published by Hope Technologies (“we”, “us”, “our”) on Google Play, why it is processed, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Our website has a separate privacy policy.

At a glance

  • Our games do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Game progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • We show ads with Google AdMob and use Google Firebase to understand how the games are played and to fix crashes. These services receive device information and identifiers such as your advertising ID.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See Deleting your data.

1. Who we are

Hope Technologies is an independent software development and mobile game publishing studio based at Walton Road, Lahore Cantt., Pakistan. We publish games on Google Play under the developer name “Hope Technologies”. We are responsible for the information processed by our games as described in this policy.

2. Games covered

This policy applies to every Hope Technologies game that links to it, including:

  • Color Conveyor (com.hopetechnologies.colorconveyor)

New games are added to this list when they are released. If a game works differently from what is described here, its Google Play listing and in-game information will say so.

3. Information we process

Information you give us. If you email or call us for support, we receive your email address or phone number, your message and anything you attach, such as screenshots.

Information stored on your device. Your game progress, coins, unlocked levels and settings (such as sound and vibration) are saved on your device. We do not receive this information. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Information collected automatically by the services built into our games:

  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, language, screen size, app version and network type.
  • Identifiers: your device’s Android advertising ID and app-instance identifiers created by Firebase.
  • Approximate location: your general region (such as your country or city), worked out from your IP address. We do not collect GPS or precise location.
  • Gameplay and usage: events such as levels started and completed, session length, and how you use game features.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and rewarded ads completed.
  • Crash and performance data: crash logs, error reports and diagnostic information.
  • Purchase information: if you buy something in a game, Google Play tells us which item was bought and gives us an order reference so we can deliver it.

4. How we use information

  • To run the game, deliver purchased items and remember your settings.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads that unlock in-game bonuses, measure their performance and prevent ad fraud.
  • To understand how players use the game so we can improve levels, difficulty and features.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To answer support requests and privacy requests.
  • To protect our games and players, and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6. Advertising and consent

Our games are free and supported by ads. Ads may be personalized, meaning chosen based on your device’s advertising ID and activity, where the law allows it and you have not opted out.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game shows a consent message from Google before ads are personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game’s settings.
  • All players: on Android you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.
  • United States: where state privacy laws give you the right to opt out of targeted advertising, you can use the Android setting above or contact us.

8. In-app purchases

Some games may offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads or buying in-game items. All payments are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms. We do not receive your payment card or bank details. Refunds are handled by Google Play according to its refund policy.

9. Children

Our games are designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A game’s content rating on Google Play, such as “Rated for 3+”, describes how suitable its content is for different ages. It does not mean the game is designed for children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us at hopesoftworks@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Data retention

  • On your device: game progress and settings stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Analytics (Firebase): user-level event data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase settings, which is no longer than 14 months. After that it is deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): kept for about 90 days.
  • Advertising (AdMob): kept by Google according to Google’s retention policy.
  • Support emails: kept for as long as we need them to resolve your request, then deleted, normally within 12 months.

11. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. We extend these choices to all players wherever possible.

To delete your data:

  1. Uninstalling the game removes all progress and settings stored on your device.
  2. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings stops ads and analytics from linking new activity to your previous ID.
  3. To ask us to delete data held by our analytics and crash-reporting services, email hopesoftworks@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”, the name of the game and the approximate date you last played. You do not need to create an account.

We will reply within 30 days. We do not ask for your name or link game data to your identity, so we may ask for details that help us find your data. Some information may be kept if the law requires it.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your request, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

12. Security and international transfers

Information sent by our games to the services above is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We limit access to our analytics and developer accounts and protect them with strong security controls. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, they use legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our games, the services they use or the law change, including before we add any new service or permission. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will let you know in the game or on our Google Play listing.
