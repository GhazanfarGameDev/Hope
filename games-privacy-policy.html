Privacy in our games

This policy explains what information is processed when you download and play mobile games published by Hope Technologies (“we”, “us”, “our”) on Google Play, why it is processed, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Our website has a separate privacy policy.

At a glance

  • Each game is different. The Games covered table shows exactly which games have ads or analytics.
  • Our games do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Game progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • Games with ads use Google AdMob, and games with analytics use Google Firebase. These services receive device information and identifiers such as your advertising ID. Games without them do not.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See Deleting your data.

3. Information we process

In all games:

  • Information stored on your device: game progress, unlocked levels, coins and settings (such as sound and vibration). We do not receive this information. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Technical information: our games are built with the Unity game engine. The engine and Google Play may process limited technical information, such as device model, Android version, app version and crash or performance data, to run the game and keep it stable.
  • Information you give us: if you email or call us for support, we receive your email address or phone number, your message and anything you attach.

Only in games with ads or analytics, the services listed in section 5 also collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, language, app version and network type.
  • Identifiers: your Android advertising ID, app set ID and app-instance identifiers created by Firebase.
  • Approximate location: your general region, worked out from your IP address. We never collect GPS or precise location.
  • Gameplay and usage: events such as levels started and completed, session length, and taps.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and rewarded ads completed.
  • Crash and performance data: crash logs and diagnostic information.

4. How we use information

  • To run the game and remember your progress and settings.
  • In games with ads: to show ads, including rewarded ads that unlock in-game bonuses, measure them and prevent ad fraud.
  • In games with analytics: to understand how players use the game so we can improve it, and to find and fix crashes.
  • To answer support and privacy requests, and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and consent

This section applies only to games with ads. Those games are free and supported by ads, which may be personalized based on your advertising ID where the law allows it and you have not opted out.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game shows a consent message from Google before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the game’s settings.
  • All players: you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • United States: where state law gives you the right to opt out of targeted advertising, use the Android setting above or contact us.

9. Children

Our games are designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A game’s content rating on Google Play, such as “Rated for 3+”, describes how suitable its content is for different ages. It does not mean the game is designed for children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us at hopesoftworks@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Data retention

  • On your device: game progress and settings stay until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Analytics (Firebase): user-level event data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase settings, no longer than 14 months, and then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): kept for about 90 days.
  • Advertising (AdMob): kept by Google according to Google’s retention policy.
  • Support emails: kept for as long as needed to resolve your request, normally no longer than 12 months.

11. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. We extend these choices to all players.

To delete your data:

  1. Uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android settings. This removes progress and settings stored on your device.
  2. In games with ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings so new activity is not linked to your previous ID.
  3. To ask us to delete analytics and crash data, support emails or anything else, email hopesoftworks@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”, the name of the game and the approximate date you last played. You do not need an account.

We will reply within 30 days. Because we do not link game data to your name, we may ask for details that help us find it. If you are unhappy with how we handled your request, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Security and international transfers

Data sent by our games to the services above is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We protect our developer and analytics accounts with strong security controls. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information. Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

13. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our games, the services they use or the law change, including before we add ads, analytics, purchases or any new permission. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will let you know in the game or on our Google Play listing.
