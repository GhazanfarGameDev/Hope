Website Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Your privacy matters to us

This policy explains how Hope Technologies (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you visit hopetechnologies.online or contact us. Looking for our games? Information processed by our mobile games is covered by our Mobile Games Privacy Policy.

Who we are

Hope Technologies is a software development and mobile game publishing studio based at Walton Road, Lahore Cantt., Pakistan. We are responsible for the information described in this policy.

Information we collect

When you contact us. If you email or call us, we receive your name, email address or phone number, any business details you share, and the content of your message.

When you browse the website. This website has no accounts, forms, ads or analytics tracking, and it does not set cookies. Like any website, it does receive some technical information automatically:

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts and icons: pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg.com. Your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address and browser details. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

How we use information

We use information to reply to your messages, provide support for our games and services, keep the website secure and working, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with the services that run our website and email (such as GitHub and Google), when the law requires it, or when needed to protect our rights, users or the public.

Data retention and security

We keep emails and messages for as long as we need them to handle your request, normally no longer than 24 months, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. The website is served over encrypted HTTPS. No online system is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, let us know and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to object to how it is used. To make a request, email us. We will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
